InstaScope is a free, open-source tool that helps you understand your Instagram audience — without ever touching your data.

Our story

InstaScope started from a simple frustration: why is it so hard to know who actually follows you back on Instagram? The native app doesn't tell you. Third-party apps ask for your password — which is both dangerous and against Instagram's Terms of Service.

The answer turned out to be hiding in plain sight. Since 2018, Instagram (like all platforms operating under GDPR) is required to let users download a full copy of their own data. That ZIP file contains your entire follower and following history — it just takes a tool to make sense of it.

InstaScope was built to be that tool. Simple, fast, and completely private. No login, no API access, no servers. Everything runs in your browser using JavaScript — and when you close the tab, it's all gone.

How it works

Instagram lets you request a full export of your account data — including your complete follower and following lists. InstaScope reads that ZIP file directly in your browser, compares the two lists, and instantly shows you:

— Who follows you but you don't follow back
— Who you follow but doesn't follow you back
— Changes between two exports over time (History tab)

There's no server involved. The file is processed by JavaScript running locally on your device. When you close the page, everything disappears — we never see it, store it, or transmit it anywhere.
